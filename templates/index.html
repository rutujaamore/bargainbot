How the verdict is built

Three separate, transparent layers - not one black box. Each one is honest about what it actually knows.

1
Deal Score
A Linear Regression model trained on thousands of real Amazon/ Kaggle prices learns the typical price for a category. Today's live price is compared against it - and if the price is outside anything the model has actually seen, it says "limited data" instead of guessing.
Genuinely trained
2
Festival Calendar
A real, hardcoded calendar of 21 known Indian sale events (Diwali, Big Billion Days, Prime Day...) with honest low-high discount ranges - not a model prediction, just a lookup of dates that are public knowledge.
Rule-based lookup
3
AI Explanation
Every number above is calculated first. Groq's Llama 3.3 only turns those already-computed facts into one plain sentence - it's never allowed to invent a price, date, or percentage.
Phrasing only
